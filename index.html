HOW TO PLAY

  • Start on the cyan star — it's always the first one.
  • Drag your finger through every white star in order.
  • Decoys appear from sigil three. Touching a decoy resets the stroke.
  • Hints show the path early — they fade at sigil four and beyond.
  • Time matters from sigil six. Lift your finger to start the stroke over.
  • Ten sigils recover ten lines of the artefact's message.

Each star you hit plays a note. A clean sigil plays a song.
