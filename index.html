HOW TO PLAY

  • Start on the cyan star — it's always the first one.
  • Drag through every white star in order.
  • Decoys from sigil three. Touching one resets the stroke.
  • Hints fade from sigil four — remember the shape.
  • Drifting stars appear in daily and deeper endless — they orbit; aim where they'll be.
  • Red stars are hostile. Your trace line must not pass within their range.
  • Modes — STORY decodes the message; DAILY is a fresh sigil each day; ENDLESS is infinite.

Each star you hit plays a note. A clean sigil plays a song.
